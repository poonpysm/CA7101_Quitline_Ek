# Content Hierarchy & Page Placement

This document defines the structural priority of content across the Quitline application, optimizing for the primary persona (Ek) who needs immediate, high-trust, and time-efficient information.

---

## 🔝 Floating / Global Overlay (Highest Priority)
*These elements persist across all viewpoints and take precedence during any sudden exposure risk.*
*   **Quick Escape Button:** A highly visible, sticky control (top-right or bottom-right corner) with a keyboard shortcut (`ESC`) that instantly loads a neutral site (e.g., `google.com`).
*   **Privacy Mode Banner:** A subtle header indicator confirming: *"Guest Mode Active — No browser tracking cookies."*

---

## 1. Above-the-Fold (Primary Landing Section)
*Goal: Build immediate trust, state the value proposition, and provide a frictionless entry point within 3 seconds.*
1.  **Value Proposition Headline:** A professional, discreet title.
    *   *Draft:* "Professional & Confidential Cessation Planning."
2.  **Call to Action (CTA) Button:** High-priority, direct tool entry.
    *   *Action:* "Build Your Quit Plan (2 Min, No Sign-up required)" — links directly to the interactive plan generator.
3.  **Core Privacy Indicator:** Subtext directly below the CTA.
    *   *Copy:* "100% anonymous. We do not store your data or log your IP address."

---

## 2. Mid-Page (Evidence-Based Planning & Coping Tools)
*Goal: Deliver instant, scannable value to support Ek's "Preparation" stage.*
1.  **The Interactive Quit Plan Generator (Embedded Tool):**
    *   A Fill-in box of target quit date and work environment stress level.
    *   Generates a clean first-week checklist immediately.
2.  **Evidence-Based "How-To" Guidelines (Direct Clinical Action Steps):**
    *   Concrete, step-by-step instructions (e.g., proper NRT dosing protocols, immediate environment clearing, behavioral substitution tactics).
    *   Clear, evidence-backed advice eliminating vague recommendations in favor of precise, actionable steps.
3.  **"Coping at Work" Checklist (Tabs/Accordion):**
    *   Divided into specific scenarios: *In a Meeting*, *Decision Point Stress*, *Social/Networking Cravings*.
    *   No long paragraphs; bullet points only.

---

## 3. Lower-Page (Direct Support Channels)
*Goal: Provide secure human assistance when the user is ready to engage, preserving control over contact details.*
1.  **Secure Live Chat Module:**
    *   A card displaying live queue status: *"Live chat available now (Anonymous & Encrypted)."*
    *   **Quick Prompt CTA Button:** *"Create My Quit Plan"* button integrated into the chat interface for instant guided plan generation.
2.  **Scheduled Call Booking System:**
    *   An embedded booking calendar allowing the user to pick an exact hour.
    *   Includes a Call Introduction selector with 2 options: *Neutral Callback Inquiry* or *Ask for me regarding...* (with optional text input box for custom topics).

---
