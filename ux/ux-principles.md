# UX Principles

These core experience principles guide all product, design, and technical decisions for the Quitline platform, ensuring alignment with the primary persona (Ek) and cohort needs.

---

## 1. Confidential by Design (Privacy First)
*   **Definition:** Privacy is a structural requirement, not an optional feature. Every design decision defaults to anonymity and local-first data persistence.
*   **In Practice:**
    *   No mandatory login walls or verification gates.
    *   User-generated quit plans are kept in browser storage rather than a central database by default.
    *   Strict integration of the floating "Quick Escape" mechanism across all viewports.

## 2. Efficiency Over Friction (Time is Currency)
*   **Definition:** Respect the user's extreme time constraints. Deliver maximum clinical and behavioral value in minimal interactions.
*   **In Practice:**
    *   Break down guides into scannable micro-learning steps (under 2 minutes).
    *   Optimize pages to load in under 1 second to eliminate loading anxiety.
    *   Design interactive builders (sliders/toggles) that deliver instant visual results without onboarding steps.

## 3. Objective & Clinical Integrity (Logical Over Emotional)
*   **Definition:** Deliver direct, science-backed guidance. Replace emotional appeals, testimonials, and alarmist imagery with evidence-based physiological checklists and timelines.
*   **In Practice:**
    *   Copywriting focuses on physiological restoration milestones (e.g., blood pressure, oxygenation rates).
    *   Provide concrete behavioral coping frameworks rather than generic motivational text.
    *   Link directly to authoritative health databases (CDC, WHO) to validate recommendations.

## 4. Discreet Styling & Neutral Presence (Zero Over-the-Shoulder Risk)
*   **Definition:** Prevent social or professional exposure. Visuals and copy should appear like a clean, professional corporate dashboard rather than a recovery or addiction platform.
*   **In Practice:**
    *   Utilize neutral corporate typography, steel-blue/slate-gray color palettes, and understated styling.
    *   Avoid using graphic images of smoking hazards or bright red alert themes.
    *   Use neutral terminology in page titles and navigation labels (e.g., "Performance Planning" or "Health Protocol").

## 5. Absolute User Agency (Control Over Engagement)
*   **Definition:** Empower the user to manage their engagement. The user has full control over how they schedule calls, interact with chat, and receive communications.
*   **In Practice:**
    *   Implement scheduling workflows where users define caller-identification masking instructions.
    *   Offer clear, single-click mechanisms to purge all cached session history in the footer.
    *   Use purely opt-in models for all communications.
