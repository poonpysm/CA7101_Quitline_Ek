# Mood & Tone Guideline

This document defines the emotional tone and visual character guidelines for the Quitline platform, ensuring the product feels professional, supportive, and secure for high-agency, privacy-minded users.

---

## 🎭 Emotional Tone (The Brand Personality)

The emotional tone dictates how the copy and interactions feel. For Ek, the platform must act as a **quiet, highly competent strategic advisor**, avoiding both medical alarmism and overly emotional hand-holding.

```
       CLINICAL / OBJECTIVE
                ▲
                │
CONFIDENTIAL ───┼─── EMPOWERING
                │
                ▼
         CALM / GROUNDED
```

*   **1. Confidential & Trustworthy (No Intrusion)**
    *   *Feeling:* Safe, anonymous, protected.
    *   *Tone Execution:* Copy explicitly reinforces security ("No local cookies are tracking your session"). Interface interactions are low-friction and never ask for personal identifiers unnecessarily.
*   **2. Clinical & Objective (Zero Fluff)**
    *   *Feeling:* Action-oriented, logical, scientific.
    *   *Tone Execution:* Use physiological data and exact timelines. Avoid guilt-based messaging, dramatic lung graphics, or moralizing. Treat nicotine dependency as a physiological challenge to be resolved strategically.
*   **3. Calm & Grounding (Reducing Anxiety)**
    *   *Feeling:* Composed, focused, stress-mitigating.
    *   *Tone Execution:* Clean layout spaces, interactive widgets that slow down interactions (like breathing loops), and smooth, non-startling transitions.
*   **4. Empowering & Self-Directed (User Agency)**
    *   *Feeling:* Respectful of the user's intelligence and autonomy.
    *   *Tone Execution:* Emphasize that the user is in total control. Offer modular guides so they can pick what works for them, customize call masking, or wipe data easily.

---

## 🎨 Visual Character (The Aesthetic Vibe)

The visual character is modern, corporate, and minimalist. It mimics high-end developer/productivity SaaS platforms (e.g., Stripe, Linear) to feel familiar and professional, allowing for discreet browsing.

*   **1. Professional SaaS Aesthetic (Minimalist & Premium)**
    *   *Layout:* Structured grids, ample border borders, clear white spaces, and distinct visual cards.
    *   *Styling:* Muted charcoal slates, deep navy background layers, subtle glassmorphism blurs, and thin borders.
*   **2. Visual Discretion (Understated Presence)**
    *   *Concept:* Safe for open offices and public commutes.
    *   *Application:* Title tags and header text read "Performance Plan" or "Health Protocol" rather than large "SMOKING CESSATION PORTAL" titles. The branding uses abstract shapes (e.g., rings or lines representing timelines) instead of cigarettes or smoke imagery.
*   **3. High-Density Scannability (Rapid Consumption)**
    *   *Typography:* Outlined fonts, high-contrast weights for headings, and short paragraph blocks.
    *   *UI Details:* Tabbed guides, lists with checkmarks, and data-focused metrics.
*   **4. Fluid Micro-Animations (Premium Tactile Feel)**
    *   *Application:* Smooth slide-ins for calculators, organic expanding rings for breathing exercises, and elegant opacity transitions for tabs. No flashy bounce animations or aggressive attention-stealers.
