# Content Requirements & Strategy

This document outlines the content strategy, messaging priorities, and constraints designed to satisfy the primary persona (Ek, the busy and privacy-focused business owner) during his preparation stage.

---

## 📋 Required Content Modules

### 1. The "Discreet Quit" Framework (How-To Guides)
*   **Step-by-Step Quit Plan Builder:** A structured guide that helps Ek define his timeline and methods. Must be interactive or highly structured.
*   **Business-Context Coping Strategies:** Targeted advice for managing cravings and withdrawal symptoms in high-stress work scenarios (e.g., "How to handle a craving during a board meeting," "Maintaining cognitive focus without nicotine").
*   **Progress Indicators:** A privacy-first tool to track milestones without exposing data (e.g., client-side state savings only).

### 2. Privacy & Security Disclosures
*   **Data Integrity Badges & Statements:** Short, clear callouts explaining that chat logs are not kept permanently, personal data is never sold, and session history can be cleared with one click.
*   **Channel Safety Guidelines:** Clear instructions on how to use Private Chat securely and what to expect when scheduling a call (e.g., "Our agents will ask for you by name without mentioning our organization's name to others").

### 3. Immediate Action Toolkits
*   **The 30-Second Craving Buster:** A micro-module of quick techniques to deal with immediate urges during work hours.
*   **Private Call Booking Interface:** A clean scheduling module with customizable intro protocols.

---

## 🎯 Messaging Priorities

1.  **Direct, Objective, and Scientific Tone:** 
    *   Avoid emotional appeals, guilt-trips, or lengthy testimonials. 
    *   Use professional, business-friendly, and clinical/evidence-based language (e.g., "efficacy," "symptom management," "strategic milestones").
2.  **Confidentiality & Control First:**
    *   Reassure the user of their anonymity immediately upon entry.
    *   *Example headline:* "Confidential cessation support, tailored for busy schedules. No accounts required."
3.  **Actionability & Efficiency:**
    *   Lead with bulleted lists, summary tables, and estimated read times (e.g., "2-min read").

---

## 🚫 Content Constraints

| Constraint | Detail & Implementation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No Mandatory Sign-ups** | Do not gate core content behind forms, email sign-ups, or verification steps. |
| **No Exposure Risk (Neutral Framing)** | Avoid conspicuous headings like "Quit Smoking Portal" in large fonts that could be easily read by coworkers looking over Ek's shoulder. Use neutral visual headers (e.g., "Professional Health Support"). |
| **Micro-Content Cap** | General content blocks must be capped at 150–200 words. Use bullet points and checklists instead of long-form paragraphs. |
| **No Tracking Trails** | Avoid automatic email follow-ups or push notifications unless explicitly opted into and configured with masked text. |
