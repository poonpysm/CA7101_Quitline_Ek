# User Research & Insights

This document synthesizes user research, interview notes, behavioral patterns, and tactical findings derived from studying busy, privacy-first professionals and business owners (represented by the primary persona, Ek) who are preparing to quit smoking.

---

## 📋 Executive Summary

Research was conducted to understand the mental models, day-to-day frictions, and structural barriers encountered by high-responsibility individuals attempting to quit smoking. The cohort values absolute discretion, high efficiency, and actionable guidance. 

Traditional cessation programs fail this group due to:
1.  **High-friction entry points** (long intake questionnaires).
2.  **Unscheduled call policies** (disruptive to meetings and client interactions).
3.  **High exposure risk** (conspicuous physical materials or notifications).

---

## 🔑 Key Research Insights

### Theme 1: "Time is Currency" (Efficiency Over Process)
*   **Insight:** Business owners and executives do not have time for general education or mandatory video/text onboarding. They treat information-gathering as a checklist.
*   **Behavioral Pattern:** Immediately navigating away from pages that require account registration or present blocks of text with low information density.
*   **Design Action:** Keep reading times under 2 minutes, use summary checklists, and make interactive tools (e.g., plan builders) instantly accessible.

### Theme 2: Reputational Exposure Anxiety (Privacy as a Hard Requirement)
*   **Insight:** Fear of being seen struggling with addiction, which could weaken leadership perception among employees or affect client trust.
*   **Behavioral Pattern:** Using private/incognito browsing windows, refusing to sign up with business emails, and expressing worry about browser history.
*   **Design Action:** Implement a highly visible "Quick Escape" function, a cookieless guest mode, and masked phone booking workflows.

### Theme 3: Command-and-Control Messaging (Direct Advice)
*   **Insight:** The target user wants clinical, tactical, and physiological guidance rather than emotional support or motivational quotes.
*   **Behavioral Pattern:** High engagement with recovery timelines (e.g., "Nicotine levels at 12 hours") and concrete craving tactics (e.g., tactical breathing, physical substitutes).
*   **Design Action:** Use objective, science-backed copy. Structure the content around milestones and physiological progress.

## 🗣️ Simulated Interview Excerpts (Persona Alignment)

> **Subject:** Entrepreneur/Business Owner, Age 47  
> **Quote:** *"If my employees see a quitting guide on my office desktop, it changes how they look at me. I need to handle this quietly, on my own schedule, during my evening commute or late at night. I don't want a quit coach calling me in the middle of a client pitch."*  
> **Finding:** High demand for asynchronous text channels and scheduled call-back slots where the user controls the exact timing and context of the contact.

> **Subject:** Managing Director, Age 43  
> **Quote:** *"I tried an app once, but it asked for my email and phone number before I could even see the features. I deleted it immediately. I don't need marketing emails on my work inbox about smoking."*  
> **Finding:** Onboarding gates are a major point of drop-off. Value must be proven *before* any registration is requested.

## 🧠 Behavioral Triggers & Mindsets

```
[Trigger Event] (e.g., High-Stress Meeting, Decision Point)
      │
      ▼
[Habit Impulse] (Crave Nicotine for stress reduction/focus)
      │
      ▼
[The Conflict] (Needs to quit but cannot risk brain fog or irritability during work)
      │
      ▼
[The Need] (Direct, 30-second cognitive coping tools + strict privacy assurance)
```

### Quitting Phase: "Preparation"
*   **Current Focus:** Gathering information, evaluating methods (cold turkey vs. NRT), and building a structured timeline.
*   **UX Objective:** Provide the user with a downloadable or browser-cached "Quit Strategy" document that summarizes their timeline, triggers, and emergency tactics in a clean, professional PDF or card layout.
