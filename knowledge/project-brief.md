# Project Brief

This project brief defines the strategic direction, audience alignment, structural boundaries, and success benchmarks for the Quitline website/web application customized for professional users.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

Create a high-trust, privacy-first, and highly efficient smoking cessation web portal tailored for busy professionals and business owners. The application enables users to research cessation strategies, design a personalized quit plan, and access secure, professional support channels (private chat/scheduled calls) without compromising their professional reputation, work performance, or digital privacy.

---

## 👥 Target Audience

*   **Primary Persona:** Ek (45, Business Owner).
*   **Segment Characteristics:**
    *   **Time-Poor:** Demands immediate, scannable, and highly structured information.
    *   **Discretion-Focused:** Values absolute confidentiality to protect client relationships, staff morale, and professional image.
    *   **Logical & Action-Oriented:** Prefers clinical, evidence-based coping guidelines, step-by-step instruction lists, and practical frameworks rather than emotional narratives.

---

## ⚙️ Project Constraints

### 1. Privacy & Security Constraints
*   **Zero Footprint:** No mandatory account creation or email validation to access core planning tools.
*   **Local-First Persistence:** User-defined plans and progress must be saved locally (e.g., in browser `localStorage`) rather than in a vulnerable central database, unless explicitly opted in.
*   **Visual Discretion:** Visual assets and page headers must be professional and clean. Large, stigmatizing images or bright banners related to smoking must be avoided to prevent accidental over-the-shoulder exposure.

### 2. Time & Performance Constraints
*   **Immediate Load Times:** High optimization is necessary for fast load times to respect the limited time of busy professionals.
*   **Mobile & Desktop Fluidity:** Layout must be responsive, enabling quick, private checks on a mobile device or discreet window management on a desktop.

---

## 🏆 Success Criteria

*   **Trust Metrics:** Successful integration of prominent privacy indicators (e.g., security badging, data retention disclaimers) leading to a high conversion rate of anonymous users booking private sessions.
*   **Frictionless Conversion:** 100% of core planning tools, timeline builders, and coping checklists remain completely ungated (accessible with zero input fields).
*   **Time-to-Value:** Users can access a step-by-step quit plan or craving-busting strategy in less than 30 seconds from landing on the homepage.
*   **Engagement Discretion:** Successful implementation of exit features (e.g., Quick Escape button) to guarantee peace of mind.
